1. Description of Service

Biblely is a faith, fitness, and productivity companion app for iPhone. It provides the following features:

  • Bible: Bible reading in 44 translations, verse saving, highlighting, bookmarks, notes, share cards, verse audio, Bible quiz, Bible mini-games, Bible timeline, Bible maps, Bible characters with audio stories, thematic study guides, key verses, and fast facts
  • Prayer: daily prayers with weekday schedules and linked verses, prayer reflections, prayer boards with folders and envelopes, stickers, photos, and backgrounds, and prayer reminders
  • Guide chat: a Bible study chat that accepts typed text, voice input, and photos of text
  • Focus: to-do list with automated priority scoring, scheduled tasks and calendar views, visions (life goals), habits with daily check-ins, an Emergency screen, distraction games, and relapse journaling, reminders on a day timeline, and My Week, a day planner that shows your Biblely items next to your iPhone Calendar events, with day templates, a weather line, and an automated "Make it fit" replanner
  • Fitness: workout logging with sets, reps, and weights, workout templates and folders, weekly split planning with muscle groups and equipment, automated workout generation, a bundled library of over 900 exercises plus your own custom exercises, YouTube tutorial links, gym history, scheduled workouts, physique tracking with a body map and per-muscle scores, body composition estimates, and a Coach chat that accepts typed text, voice input, and gym photos
  • Nutrition: food logging with photo and barcode scanning, calorie and macronutrient tracking, favourite meals, personalised daily targets, and automated nutrition plans
  • Calendar sync: optional writing of prayers, reminders, tasks, workouts, and day template blocks to Biblely calendars on your iPhone
  • Customisation: 25+ visual themes, wallpapers, alternate app icons, loading and streak animations, and tab bar and card layout options
  • Achievements: points, levels, streaks, and achievement badges across all app features
  • Journal: personal journaling with a calendar view and verse-linked notes
  • Widgets: home screen widgets for the daily verse, habits, body composition, tasks, My Week, nutrition, and visions

The app is free of charge. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or ads. Referring friends can unlock cosmetic extras (share card backgrounds, extra reading voices, and some themes and animations). No payment is ever involved.

Biblely has no social features. There is no feed, friends list, messaging between users, or leaderboard, and nothing you create is shown to other users.
