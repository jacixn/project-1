Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Biblely, developed and operated by Jason ("we", "our", or "the app"), is a faith, fitness, and productivity companion for iPhone, available at biblely.uk. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, who we share it with, and your rights.

Biblely has no social features. There is no feed, no friends list, no messaging between users, and no leaderboard. Nothing you create in the app is shown to other users.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Account Information

Biblely requires an account. When you create one we collect your email address, a username, a display name and a password. Sign-in is by email or username plus password, handled by Firebase Authentication. We do not use Sign in with Apple, Google sign-in, or any social login.

During setup you can also choose a preset avatar, a country from a list (we do not use your location for this), a theme, a Bible translation, your weight and height units, and a language. You may be asked how you heard about Biblely and what you want to get out of it. These choices are saved to your account record.

Your account record also holds whether your email is verified, whether two-factor sign-in is turned on, your referral details (the username that referred you and how many people you have referred), and the dates you joined and were last active.

We do not ask for your age or gender when you create an account. Age and gender are only asked for in the Nutrition body profile (see Section 1.3), and only if you use that feature.

1.2 Content You Create

Content you create inside the app, including:

  • Personal prayers, their weekday schedules, and your completion history
  • Prayer boards, including folders and envelopes, stickers, photos, backgrounds, and board layouts
  • Journal entries, verse notes, bookmarks, highlights, saved verses, and recent Bible searches
  • To-do items, scheduled tasks, and task descriptions
  • Visions (life goals with target dates) and the reflections you add to them
  • Habits, daily check-ins, and any notes you write on the "I Relapsed" screen
  • Reminders and day templates
  • Workout logs, workout templates and folders, split plans, available equipment, and custom exercises you add
  • Food logs and favourite meals
  • Messages you send in the Guide (Bible) chat and the Coach (fitness) chat. Guide chat history is stored on your device and, when you are signed in, in your account record in Firebase so it follows you across devices. Coach chat history stays on your device

This content is stored on your device. When you are signed in, the following is also saved to your account in Firebase (see Section 3.3) so it follows you across devices: prayers, their schedules and completion history; prayer boards; journal entries, verse notes, bookmarks, highlights, saved verses and recent Bible searches; to-do items, scheduled tasks and task descriptions; visions and their reflections; habits, check-ins and relapse notes; reminders; workout logs, workout templates and folders, and split plans; food logs and favourite meals; and Guide chat history.

A subset of this is also backed up to your iCloud (see Section 3.4): verse notes and highlights, saved verses, journal entries, reading streaks, achievements, highlight names, to-dos and completed to-dos, stats (points and level), Bible version, units, prayer history and completions, your profile, app settings, and the onboarding flag.

Day templates, custom exercises, available equipment, and Coach chat history stay on your device only. Biblely does not sync them to Firebase or back them up to iCloud.

Habit check-ins and relapse notes can be sensitive. They are handled like the rest of your content and are never shown to anyone else.

1.3 Health and Fitness Data

Workout history and exercise logs (sets, reps, weights), split plans (training days, muscle groups, exercise counts), available equipment, and physique scores. Physique scores (per-muscle scores, balance suggestions, training frequency) are calculated on your device from your workout history.

If you use Nutrition, you can enter a body profile: gender, age, height, weight, body fat percentage, target weight, goal, and activity level. From this the app works out daily calorie and macronutrient targets and body composition estimates (BMI, body fat, muscle mass, visceral fat, body water, and body age) using formulas on your device. Your food log stores what you ate and its calories and macronutrients.

Weigh-ins are typed in by hand. Biblely does not connect to Bluetooth scales and does not read or write Apple Health.

1.4 Chat, Voice, and Photos Sent to AI Services

The Guide chat and the Coach chat accept typed text, voice input from your microphone, and photos. Other features (verse simplification, prayer reflections, task scoring, smart workout generation, physique coach feedback, nutrition plans, and the "Make it fit" replanner in My Week) also send text to outside AI services to generate a reply. Section 4 lists each service, what is sent, and where it goes.

1.5 Usage and Progress Data

Streaks, points, level, achievements, and counters such as prayers completed, workouts completed, tasks completed, quizzes taken, verses read, Bible characters read, timeline eras viewed, and maps visited. These are stored on your device and, if you are signed in, in your account record so your progress follows you across devices. We also count how many AI requests you make each day, per feature, to enforce fair-use limits.

1.6 Notifications and Push Tokens

Prayer reminders, task and habit reminders, workout reminders, the Saturday weigh-in reminder, achievement alerts, and streak reminders are scheduled by the app on your device as local notifications. They do not go through a server. You control them in Settings and in iOS notification settings.

When you sign in, the app also requests an Expo push token and stores it in your account record. We do not currently send any remote push notifications, so this token is not used to message you. The app checks whether it is running on a real device before requesting the token. We do not collect device identifiers for tracking.

1.7 Photos and Camera

If you choose to use the camera or photo library, images are used as follows:

  • Profile photo: if your email is verified, you can upload a custom photo. It is first sent to Google Gemini for automated content moderation (nudity, violence, hate symbols, and similar). If it passes, it is uploaded to Firebase Storage and the link is saved in your profile. If it fails, nothing is stored and you are told why. You get three attempts. After a third rejected photo, and after each accepted upload, you cannot upload another custom photo until the following day (the cooldown ends at midnight). Rejected photos are not stored.
  • Food photos: sent to Google Gemini to estimate calories and macronutrients. We do not store them on our servers. If you type a food name instead of taking a photo, that text is sent to Google Gemini to estimate its nutrition.
  • Gym photos in the Coach chat: sent to Google Gemini for analysis. The text description Gemini returns is then sent to the text AI providers in Section 4.3 to write the Coach's reply. We do not store the photo on our servers.
  • Photos of text in the Guide chat: sent to OCR.space to extract the words, which are then sent to the text AI providers.
  • Prayer board photos and backgrounds: uploaded to Firebase Storage in a folder tied to your account so your boards sync across devices.
  • Barcode scanning: the camera reads the barcode on your device. Only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts.
  • Habits "Emergency" screen: shows your front camera as a live mirror. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Saving to Photos: share cards and prayer board images can be saved to your photo library if you allow it.

1.8 Calendar

If you allow calendar access, My Week, the day timeline, and day templates read events from your iPhone calendars (all event calendars, including those from other apps) to show them next to your Biblely items and let you move them. This reading happens on your device. Calendar events are not uploaded to our servers.

If you turn on calendar sync in Settings, the app creates a "Biblely" calendar and a "Biblely Work" calendar on your iPhone and writes your prayers, reminders, tasks, workouts, and day template blocks to them, with the default alarm you choose. Turning sync off deletes the Biblely and Biblely Work calendars and the events Biblely wrote to them.

If you use "Make it fit" in My Week, the titles and times of the items on that day, which can include your calendar events, are sent to the text AI providers in Section 4.3 to suggest a new order. You review the suggestion before anything changes.

1.9 Weather

My Week can show a weather line. If you type a city name, that name is sent to Open-Meteo to look up its coordinates, and the coordinates are then sent to Open-Meteo for the forecast. The city you typed is stored on your device only. Biblely never uses your device location.

1.10 Preferences and Customisation

Your theme (from 25+ themes), wallpaper, app icon, loading and streak animations, tab bar and card layout, notification toggles and alert style, notification sound, calendar sync setting, units, Bible translation, language, reading voice, and referral status. These are stored on your device. Some of these (theme, dark mode and wallpaper, loading and streak animations, notification preferences, prayer preferences, Bible translation, reading progress, recent Bible searches, units and country) are also saved to your account record in Firebase. Only your Bible translation and units are also backed up to iCloud. Your app icon, tab bar layout, and card order stay on your device.

1.11 Home Screen Widgets

The Daily Verse, Habits, Body Composition, Tasks, Add Task, My Week, Fuel Tracker, and Vision widgets read a copy of your data from a shared container on your device. Widgets do not use the network.

1.12 Device Information

The app reads your device language and region to pick a locale, and checks whether it is running on a physical device. It does not request your location, contacts, Bluetooth, motion data, or Apple Health, and it does not use the advertising identifier.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the app's features (Bible reading and study, prayers, prayer boards, journal, tasks, visions, habits, reminders, My Week, workouts, physique, nutrition, and Coach and Guide chat)
  • To sync your data across your devices through Firebase and iCloud when you are signed in
  • To schedule the local reminders and alerts you turn on
  • To generate replies, suggestions, scores, reflections, and plans using the outside AI services listed in Section 4
  • To turn your voice into text for chat, and to read verses, prayers, and chat replies aloud
  • To read text from photos (OCR) in the Guide chat
  • To show and move your calendar events in My Week, and to write Biblely items to your calendar when you turn on calendar sync
  • To show a weather forecast for the city you type
  • To send you email codes for verification, password reset, and two-factor sign-in
  • To track your points, streaks, and achievements
  • To enforce daily fair-use limits on AI features
  • To run the referral system and unlock cosmetic items
  • To open YouTube search links for exercise tutorials

3. Data Storage and Retention

3.1 Local Storage

Your data is stored on your device so the app works offline. Completed tasks, prayer completion history, quiz history, food logs and workout history older than 90 days are removed automatically, on the device and in the cloud. The exercise library is bundled inside the app and is not downloaded.

3.2 Local Caches

  • Bible cache: downloaded Bible translation data is cached on your device for up to 30 days for offline reading.
  • Text-to-speech audio cache: generated audio for verses, prayers, and chat replies is cached on your device for up to 1 year to avoid repeat processing.
  • Weather cache: the forecast for your typed city is cached for 45 minutes.

3.3 Cloud Storage (Firebase)

When you are signed in, your data, including your Guide chat history, is saved to Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage) on servers located in the United States so it can follow you across devices. Cloud data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all of your cloud data is permanently removed.

3.4 iCloud Sync

If your iPhone is signed in to iCloud, Biblely automatically backs up part of your app data to your private iCloud container (iCloud Drive files and iCloud key-value storage): verse notes and highlights, saved verses, journal entries, reading streaks, achievements, highlight names, to-dos and completed to-dos, stats (points and level), Bible version, units, prayer history and completions, your profile, app settings, and the onboarding flag. The rest of your content syncs through Firebase only (Section 3.3) or stays on your device (Section 1.2). This happens whenever iCloud is available; there is no separate switch inside the app. You can manage iCloud for Biblely in iOS Settings. iCloud data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, and Biblely removes it when you delete your account.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following services to run the app. Requests go directly from your device to each service. We do not run a server in between. Apart from Guide chat history, which is saved to your account record so it syncs across devices (see Section 1.2), we do not keep copies of what is sent to the AI, speech, or OCR services. Data shared with each service is limited to what the feature needs.

4.1 Google Firebase

Used for account sign-in (email and password), the cloud database that stores your synced data, file storage for profile photos and prayer board images, and small server functions that email your codes, handle two-factor sign-in, roll back referrals on account deletion, and count daily AI usage. Data is stored on servers in the United States.

Firebase Privacy Policy

4.2 Apple

iCloud for automatic backup (Section 3.4), the iPhone Calendar for My Week and calendar sync (Section 1.8), Photos for saving and picking images, Apple Maps for the Bible maps (with location turned off), WidgetKit for home screen widgets, and the App Store review prompt.

Apple Privacy Policy

4.3 AI Text Providers

To generate text replies, the app sends your request to one of the following providers, chosen automatically based on which one is available at that moment. If one is busy or out of capacity, the app moves to the next.

  • Groq (privacy policy)
  • Cerebras (privacy policy)
  • SambaNova (privacy policy)
  • DeepSeek (privacy policy)
  • OpenRouter, which routes to Meta Llama, Google Gemma, Mistral, and DeepSeek models hosted by other companies (privacy policy)
  • Mistral AI (privacy policy)
  • Google Gemini (privacy policy, Gemini API terms)

Depending on the feature, the request can include: your chat messages and recent conversation, your display name (used to greet you), Bible verse text and references, your prayer text, task text, the titles and times of your day's items for "Make it fit", nutrition profile numbers (gender, age, height, weight, body fat percentage, target weight, goal, activity level), calorie targets, workout summaries and exercise names, physique scores, and body composition estimates. We never send your email address, password, or sign-in tokens.

Each provider processes the text to produce a reply and handles it under its own privacy policy. Some providers may keep requests for abuse monitoring or, on free tiers, to improve their models, according to their terms. We do not control that. Apart from Guide chat history, which is saved to your account record so it syncs across devices (see Section 1.2), we do not store the requests or replies on our servers.

Important: DeepSeek is operated by a company based in the People's Republic of China. Text sent to DeepSeek may be processed on servers in China. By using AI features you acknowledge that your request may be handled by any provider in this list.

4.4 Google Gemini (Images)

Used for food photo nutrition estimates, Coach gym photo analysis, and custom profile photo moderation. Only the photo and a fixed analysis instruction are sent to Google; any caption you type is sent to the text providers in Section 4.3 together with Gemini's description. If you type a food name instead of taking a photo, that text is sent to Google Gemini to estimate its nutrition. We do not store these photos on our servers. Google handles them under its privacy policy and the Gemini API terms linked above.

4.5 Speech-to-Text

When you use the microphone in the Guide or Coach chat, the app records a short audio clip and sends it to the first available provider in this order: Groq (Whisper), Mistral AI (Voxtral), Google Gemini, then Google Cloud Speech-to-Text. The provider returns the transcript, which is then sent to the text providers in Section 4.3 as your message. The audio clip is not kept by us. Each provider handles the audio under its own policy (see the links in Section 4.3 and Google Cloud Speech-to-Text data logging).

4.6 Google Cloud Text-to-Speech

Used to read verses, prayers, and chat replies aloud with the cloud voices in the voice picker. The text is sent to Google Cloud for speech synthesis and the audio is cached on your device. Bible character audio stories are pre-recorded files downloaded from GitHub, or, if a file is missing, generated from the story text by Google Cloud Text-to-Speech. They are not generated from your data. The app can also use the built-in iOS voices, which stay on your device.

Google Cloud Text-to-Speech data logging, Google Cloud Privacy Notice

4.7 OCR.space

Used to read text from photos you attach in the Guide chat. The image is sent to OCR.space and the extracted text is returned.

OCR.space Privacy Policy

4.8 Open Food Facts

Used for barcode food lookup. Only the barcode number is sent. Open Food Facts is a free, open database of food products.

Open Food Facts Terms of Use

4.9 Open-Meteo

Used for the weather line in My Week. The city name you type is sent to look up coordinates, and the coordinates are sent to fetch a 7-day forecast. No account details are sent.

Open-Meteo Terms and Privacy

4.10 GitHub

Bible translations, quiz questions, Bible character profiles, and character audio stories are downloaded from GitHub. Your device fetches these files directly. No account details or content of yours is sent to GitHub.

GitHub Privacy Statement

4.11 Resend

Used to email your 6-digit codes for email verification, password reset, and two-factor sign-in, from noreply@biblely.uk. Your email address and the code are shared with Resend for this purpose only.

Resend Privacy Policy

4.12 Expo

The app is built with Expo. When you sign in, it requests a push token from Expo's push service and stores it in your account record. We do not currently send push notifications, so no notification content is shared with Expo.

Expo Privacy Policy

4.13 YouTube

The exercise library offers tutorial links. When you tap one, your device opens YouTube (the app or website) with a search for the exercise name. The app sends nothing to YouTube itself. Your use of YouTube is governed by YouTube's own policies.

YouTube (Google) Privacy Policy

4.14 EyeCandy (Same Developer)

If you also have EyeCandy, another app by the same developer, installed, the two apps can pass a request between them on your device using app links (for example, EyeCandy asking Biblely to move a reminder). Biblely asks you to confirm before it changes anything. This happens entirely on your device; nothing is sent over the internet.

5. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside your country of residence, including:

  • United States: Firebase (Google) for sign-in, database, and file storage; Google (Gemini, Text-to-Speech, Speech-to-Text); Groq, Cerebras, SambaNova, and OpenRouter for AI text; Expo; GitHub.
  • People's Republic of China: DeepSeek for AI text.
  • European Union: Mistral AI (France) for AI text and speech-to-text.

Other providers in Section 4 may process data in further countries under their own policies. Where data is transferred internationally, we rely on the data protection practices of our service providers and their compliance with applicable frameworks. For AI providers we limit what is sent to what the feature needs and never send account credentials or sign-in tokens.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising. Data is only shared with the services listed in Section 4, solely to provide app features. Your content is never shown to other Biblely users. We do not sell your personal information as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or any other applicable privacy law.

7. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier is collected. The only usage counting we do is the per-day AI request count in your account record, used to enforce fair-use limits.

8. Your Rights

8.1 General Rights (All Users)

  • Access: you can view most of your data within the app; contact us at the email below for a full copy of your account record.
  • Rectification: you can correct or update your information in the app's Profile and Settings screens.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account and all associated data from Settings > Delete Account. This removes your data from Firebase, Firebase Storage, your iCloud container, and your device.
  • Portability: your data is stored on your device and is included in standard device backups.
  • Restriction of processing: you can ask us to limit how we process your personal data by contacting us at the email below.
  • Objection: you have the right to object to the processing of your personal data. Contact us at the email below to exercise this right.

8.2 Rights for Users in the EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR:

  • Legal basis for processing: we process your personal data on the following bases:
    • Legitimate interest: for core app functionality such as syncing your data, scheduling local reminders, and providing Bible reading features.
    • Consent: for optional features that send your text, voice, or photos to the AI, speech, OCR, and weather services in Section 4, and for calendar access. You can withdraw consent at any time by not using the feature or by revoking the permission in iOS Settings.
    • Contract performance: to provide the services you request when you create an account.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: you may complain to your local data protection authority if you believe your data has been processed unlawfully.
  • Data transfers: when your data leaves the EEA or UK (see Section 5), we rely on the safeguards described in that section.

8.3 Rights for California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know: you may ask us to disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources, the purpose, and the categories of third parties we share it with.
  • Right to delete: you may request deletion of your personal information, directly in the app via Settings > Delete Account, or by contacting us.
  • Right to opt out of sale: we do not sell your personal information, and never have.
  • Non-discrimination: we will not treat you differently for exercising any CCPA right.

Categories of personal information we collect: identifiers (email address, username, display name), health and fitness data (workouts, physique scores, nutrition profile and food logs, weigh-ins, body composition estimates), content you create (prayers, prayer boards, journal entries, verse notes, tasks, visions, habits and relapse notes, reminders, day templates, chat messages), audio (voice input, sent for transcription only), photos (when you choose to use the camera or photo library), calendar events (read on your device), and device information (push token, locale).

"Do Not Sell My Personal Information": Biblely does not sell, and has never sold, the personal information of its users to any third party for money or other valuable consideration.

9. Children's Privacy

Biblely is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it from our servers. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us at biblelyios@gmail.com so we can act.

10. Security

We use Firebase Authentication for sign-in, encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all traffic, and optional two-factor sign-in by emailed code. Passwords are hashed by Firebase; we cannot see them. All data sent to the services in Section 4 travels over encrypted connections. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
